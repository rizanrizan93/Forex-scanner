Current articles or handpicked historical crises must not be relabelled as past features. Robustness:exact-tick selected replay, matched risk budget, yearly resets, walk-forward selection using prior years only, five-day block bootstrap, multiplicity adjustment across the full screened family, cost/slippage sensitivity, and separate compounding. No strategy is called robust because of higher leverage, a short profitable subperiod or one selected positive result.

## Expected-return model extension

After the negative initial screen, test four expected-net-R models:ridge(alpha100) and shallow histogram gradient boosting(80iterations,5leaves,minleaf40,L2=20), each with PRICE or PRICE+PIT_MACRO_NEWS features. The entry opportunity is fixed08:05WIB, SL1.25H1ATR/TP2R/flat13:00. Paired long/short outcome labels come from actually executed, cost-inclusive annual-reset tick simulations, not candle guesses. Numeric features use only completed bars, fresh EUR observations and verified snapshots/releases.

For genuinely causal predictions, models trained on2019 predict2020; models trained2019–2020 predict2021; models trained2019–2021 are frozen for2022–March2026. Every training target must have exited before the model cutoff. No model is fitted on the validation or test outcomes. Ridge scaling is fitted only on its training rows. Predictions are expected netR, not calibrated win probabilities. Choose the side with higher expectedR and trade only above a fixed threshold0.05/0.10/0.20R, giving12model experiments. PRICE has trend/distance,RSI/ADX,pre-Asia/NY/opening moves,EUR pressure,ATR/spread and weekday. FULL adds known nominal/real-yield changes,real-yield level and past-release indicator. No revised latest-series values or future event actuals/consensus.
