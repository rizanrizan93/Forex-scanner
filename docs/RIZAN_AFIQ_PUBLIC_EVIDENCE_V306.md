# V306 — Afiq Public-Reference Semantics: KEY vs Roadblock

Public archive evidence observed on 30 Sep 2026 clarifies an important distinction that V303 did not model explicitly.

The public forecast sequence describes:

- H4 bias as strongly bearish while price is reacting from supply;
- an upper H1 breakout key around 4212 used as short invalidation/exit logic;
- 4183.85 as a prior resistance that became base support after breakout;
- that 4183.85 support as a **roadblock**, not the final destination;
- lower demand as the active sell objective;
- a later recovery path with a far upside range around 4261–4297.

This means an Afiq-style map contains at least three different price objects:

1. **decision/reaction zone** — where rejection or acceptance is decided;
2. **invalidation KEY** — a level whose H1 acceptance changes or cancels the active bias;
3. **roadblock KEY** — an intermediate S/R-flip level that can interrupt price without changing the destination thesis.

V303 currently models (1) and a zone-boundary form of (2), but not an explicit (3).

## V306 diagnostic additions

Before changing live structural logic, V306 records enough evidence to quantify the gap:

- the causal V304 RIZAN baseline geometry is persisted in compact form;
- the public-reference corpus now distinguishes the 4183.85 roadblock and 4212 invalidation semantics;
- V304 alignment measures distance from the RIZAN primary route to the reference roadblock.

This measurement is research-only. It does not change V182, V229, V280, admission, or execution.

## Promotion criterion for a future roadblock engine

A roadblock engine should only be promoted after we can show across multiple causally timestamped public forecasts that adding S/R-flip levels improves:

- roadblock price error;
- route ordering;
- target/destination preservation after roadblock reaction;
- false-remap rate;
- reversal-vs-continuation classification.

One screenshot/reference is sufficient to define the semantic contract, but not to claim accuracy.
