# XAU pre-touch research plans

The optional prealert payload publishes fixed entry/SL/TP geometry separately from legacy EMA V05 confirmation. BUY uses a closed three-candle M15 FVG pullback, H1/H4 alignment and H4 volatility expansion. It plans a distal limit, TP 25 USD/oz and SL 20 USD/oz, TTL four hours, all hours tested. Historical retrospective review: 57 trades, 59.65% full TP, PF 1.804; 2x modeled costs PF 1.672; Q1 2026 19 trades, PF 1.690. The impulse SL15 candidate was rejected after its Q1 PF fell to 0.409. This is a small, post-hoc research sample, not independent validation. SELL has not passed the same gates and remains explicitly unavailable.

Geometry and first publication time stay fixed for each candidate. Any first observed touch before publication is missed. Consumer-side validation expires cached plans, rejects future timestamps and hides actionable prices when quote/history checks fail. A touch never claims a broker position or a fresh market entry. Pending plans do not replace the legacy V05 engine.

The source publisher still runs on its existing five-minute cadence; a ten-second screen refresh does not provide new quotes. Missing or late publication can miss fast first touches. Native H4 context is derived at UTC boundaries from contiguous native H1 data. Operational native/replay parity has not been established. The data collection cutoff prevents processing delay from completing a bar that was still forming at capture time.

No order API, position access, token renewal, cBot bridge operation, account binding change or workflow cadence change is added. Only read-only research snapshot/UI code is changed.
